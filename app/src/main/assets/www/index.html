PREFACE TO FOURTH EDITION

The Hymn Book of the GKS, the Theocratic Songs of Praise (TSP) has come a long way. The first compilation of the TSP published in the late 1940s, consisted of popular hymns that did not offend the faith of the Church, based on the Holy Bible

However, a new edition with a completely different set of songs and with original wordings was published in 1954, while Brother J.T. Jas Amayigha was the Choirmaster-General. However, in course of time, a number of the hymns fell into disuse as the tunes became unfamiliar to several members. The unfamiliar hymns had to be removed when a new edition was published in 1971, entitled Selected Hymns from the Theocratic Songs of Praise. Since then, the hymnbook has continued to serve as a means of exalting the name of God Almighty, for expressing thanks to Him for His goodness and for offering supplications and prayers to Him.

Be it as it may, the need for a wider array of songs for divine worship became apparent in view of the church growth in terms of geographical spread and membership strength. It was for this reason the Lord's Ministry decided to bring back the old songs that were removed from the original GKS Hymn Book. Other improvements have also been made: the songs are now arranged serially; typographical errors have been corrected and the expression, "crown of life" which was used loosely in the former editions to mean salvation for all believers, has been changed to the proper term, "eternal life". Also, Psalm 136 has been added and the rest of the psalms edited and put in consecutive order. In God's due time, other hymns that had been composed over time would be added to the Hymn Book.

We believe that this effort will go a long way in pereserving all the hymns of the Church, enable members to have wider variety of songs to suit just about every mood and circumstances and help them to better internalize the ingredients of their faith by the grace of God.
We pray God Almighty to continue to help us, through efforts like this, by his grace, to better fulfill the purpose for which we have been created, which is to give praise to him. As it is written:

"O come, let us sing unto the LORD: let us make a joyful noise to the rock of our salvation. Let us come before his presence with thanksgiving, and make a joyful noise unto him with psalms." For the LORD is a great God, and a great King above all gods." - Psalm 95:1-3.
